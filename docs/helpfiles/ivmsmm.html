Title



     ivmsmm -- Multiplicative structural mean model



Syntax



        ivmsmm depvar [varlist1] (varlist2 = varlist_iv) [if] [in] [weight] [, estmethod(string)
              log noirr level(#) options]



    varlist1 is the list of exogenous variables.



    varlist2 is the list of endogenous variables.



    varlist_iv is the list of exogenous variables used with varlist1 as instruments for varlist2.



    options               Description
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
      estmethod(string)   Estimation method: gmmalt (the default), gmm, tsls, or tslsalt
      noirr               Do not display exponentiated estimates
      log                 Show the GMM iteration log
      level(#)            Set confidence level; default is level(95)
      options             Options passed to ivpoisson (gmmalt), gmm (gmm), or ivregress (tsls and
                            tslsalt)



Description



    ivmsmm implements the multiplicative structural mean model (MSMM) of Robins (1989), which
    estimates a causal risk ratio for a binary outcome, using the estimation methods described by
    Clarke et al. (2015). These are the same methods as provided by the msmm() function in the R
    package OneSampleMR, and give the same estimates.



    With estmethod(gmmalt), the default, ivmsmm is a wrapper to ivpoisson with the multiplicative
    option, which performs generalized method of moments (GMM) estimation of the moment conditions
    E[(1, Z')'{Y exp(-b0 - psi X) - 1}] = 0.  With estmethod(gmm), ivmsmm uses gmm to fit the
    moment conditions E[(1, Z')'{Y exp(-psi X) - E[Y(0)]}] = 0, where E[Y(0)] is the mean of the
    exposure-free potential outcome.  With estmethod(tsls) and estmethod(tslsalt), which require a
    binary outcome and a single binary exposure and do not allow covariates, the MSMM is fitted by
    two-stage least squares, using ivregress, of transformed variables: Y(1 - X) on YX for tsls
    and YX on Y(1 - X) for tslsalt.  The causal risk ratio is then -1/b and -b respectively, where
    b is the coefficient of the transformed exposure, and its confidence interval is obtained from
    the delta method standard error of the log causal risk ratio.



    The equivalence of the models of Robins (1989) and Mullahy (1997), which was implemented in 
    ivpois, Nichols (2007) and ivpoisson with the mult option, and discussed in detail by
    Windmeijer and Santos Silva (1997) and Windmeijer (2002), was shown by Palmer (2011). These
    models produce moment conditions equivalent to gamma regression Dukes and Vansteelandt (2018).



Options



    estmethod(string) specifies the estimation method, one of gmmalt (the default), gmm, tsls, or
        tslsalt; see the Description. With gmm the GMM fit uses an identity initial weight matrix
        and centered moments in the optimal weight matrix (winitial(identity) center), as in the R
        package OneSampleMR.



    noirr specifies that the exponentiated estimates (causal risk ratios) are not displayed.



    log shows the GMM iteration log.



    level(#); see [R] estimation options.



    Other options are passed to ivpoisson, gmm, or ivregress depending on estmethod().



Examples



    Read in binary outcome data; y outcome, x exposure, w covariate, z* instrumental variables
    (genotypes).



        . use https://raw.githubusercontent.com/remlapmot/ivonesamplemr/main/data/ivbinoutdata,
            clear



    Fit the model with a single instrumental variable.



        . ivmsmm y (x = z1)



    Fit the model with multiple instruments.



        . ivmsmm y (x = z1 z2 z3)
        . estat overid



    Adjusting for w.



        . ivmsmm y w (x = z1 z2 z3)



    Fit the model with multiple exposures, and instruments, and adjusting for w.



        . ivmsmm y w (x1 x2 = z1 z2 z3)
        . estat overid



    Fit the moment conditions E[(1, Z')'{Y exp(-psi X) - E[Y(0)]}] = 0.



        . ivmsmm y w (x = z1 z2 z3), estmethod(gmm)
        . estat overid



    Two-stage least squares methods for a binary exposure (here a dichotomized version of x).



        . gen xbin = x > 0
        . ivmsmm y (xbin = z1 z2 z3), estmethod(tsls)
        . ivmsmm y (xbin = z1 z2 z3), estmethod(tslsalt)



    Comparison with ivpois (specify onestep option to ivmsmm/ivpoisson for equivalence).



        . ivpois y, endog(x) exog(z1) eform(RR)
        . ivpois y, endog(x) exog(z1 z2 z3) eform(RR)
        . ivpois y w, endog(x) exog(z1 z2 z3) eform(RR)
        . ivpois y w, endog(x1 x2) exog(z1 z2 z3) eform(RR)



Stored results



    With estmethod(gmmalt) please see ivpoisson##results, and with estmethod(gmm) please see 
    gmm##results. With estmethod(tsls) and estmethod(tslsalt) the results of ivregress are stored
    in e(), together with:



    Scalars        
      e(crr)              causal risk ratio
      e(crr_lb)           lower confidence limit of the causal risk ratio
      e(crr_ub)           upper confidence limit of the causal risk ratio
      e(logcrr_se)        delta method standard error of the log causal risk ratio



    In all cases e(estmethod) contains the estimation method.
