Title



     iv -- Instrumental variable (IV) programs useful for one-sample Mendelian randomization (MR)
analyses



Syntax



        iv subcommand ...



    where subcommand is one of



    fsw         ivfsw
    lsmm        ivlsmm
    msmm        ivmsmm
    mw          ivmw
    tsps        ivtsps
    tsri        ivtsri
    xtile       ivxtile
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------



    and ... is the syntax of that command, including any weights, if, in, and options.



Description



    iv is a simple wrapper to the commands in the ivonesamplemr package.



    The subcommand is specified as the ivonesamplemr program name without its iv prefix, i.e.
    ivlsmm ... can alternatively be run using the syntax iv lsmm ....  Everything after the
    subcommand is passed unchanged to that command.



Options



    iv takes the options for the specified subcommand.



Examples



        . use https://raw.githubusercontent.com/remlapmot/ivonesamplemr/main/data/ivbinoutdata,
            clear



    Fit the model with a single instrumental variable.



        . iv lsmm y (x = z1)



        . iv msmm y (x = z1 z2 z3)



        . iv tsps y (x = z1 z2 z3), link(logit)



        . iv tsri y (x = z1 z2 z3), link(logit)



    Sanderson-Windmeijer conditional F-statistics after ivregress.



        . ivregress 2sls y (x1 x2 = z1 z2 z3)



        . iv fsw



    Moving window and quantile analyses.



        . iv mw, window(2400) par(x): ivregress 2sls y (x = z1 z2 z3)



        . iv xtile, nq(4) par(x): ivregress 2sls y (x = z1 z2 z3)



Stored results



    iv returns the results from the specified subcommand.
